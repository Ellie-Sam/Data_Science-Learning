# The Benefits Loop: prompt and voiceover script for ChatGPT

Copy everything in the box below into ChatGPT (or Sora). The script runs about 60 seconds when read at a calm pace.

---

```
Create a 60-second vertical explainer video (9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps) with a calm, warm,
clear voiceover and matching on-screen captions.

AUDIENCE: General public, policymakers, and social service staff. Plain language,
8th grade reading level. No jargon.

STYLE: Flat, clean, calm 2D vector animation. Off-white background, dark gray text,
teal for "family", coral for "costs and dependence", gray for neutral things.
Simple flat icons only: family, house, dollar, paycheck, wallet, government building,
chain links. No real people, no logos, no stock photos. Large sans-serif captions in
sentence case. Smooth fades and slides, no flashing. Soft background music, quiet
under the voice.

PERSISTENT HEADER (top of screen, all scenes):
"The Benefits Loop"
"How eligibility rules can keep families dependent"
"A hypothesis worth data analysis"

Use the voiceover below word for word. Show the same words as captions.

SCENE 1 (0 to 5 s)
Voice: "Let's start with three questions."
Visual: three question-mark cards slide in.

SCENE 2 (5 to 10 s)
Voice: "Is it ethical to turn down a raise to keep government assistance, like food stamps or Medicaid?"
Visual: a paycheck with an up arrow labeled "Raise", a hand pushes it away.

SCENE 3 (10 to 14 s)
Voice: "Is it ethical to have another child to stay eligible for government assistance?"
Visual: a family icon grows by one child.

SCENE 4 (14 to 18 s)
Voice: "Is it ethical to hide your income from the government?"
Visual: a paycheck slides behind a curtain.

SCENE 5 (18 to 24 s)
Voice: "Here is a hypothesis worth testing with data: the rules for government assistance can trap families in a loop."
Visual: a circle with six empty steps draws itself.

SCENE 6 (24 to 29 s)
Voice: "Government assistance, like food stamps and Medicaid, is based on household size and income."
Visual: family icon, a plus sign, and a paycheck icon.

SCENE 7 (29 to 33 s)
Voice: "Some families believe a bigger household keeps them eligible."
Visual: family icon grows by one, a "+1" bubble.

SCENE 8 (33 to 37 s)
Voice: "Others turn down raises or hide earnings to stay under the income limit."
Visual: income bar drops below a dashed coral line labeled "Income limit". A card says "Raise offered" then a "Declined" stamp.

SCENE 9 (37 to 43 s)
Voice: "But a child costs about 860 dollars a month. Food stamps add at most about 200."
Visual: two bars grow. Short teal bar "$200, food stamps added". Tall coral bar "$860, cost of a child".
Small note: "Food stamps (SNAP) FY2026, maximum at zero income. USDA cost estimate, 2015 dollars."

SCENE 10 (43 to 46 s)
Voice: "Same income, higher costs. Families fall further behind."
Visual: a wallet empties. "Income: same" bar stays flat, "Costs: up" bar grows.

SCENE 11 (46 to 50 s)
Voice: "They depend more on government, and government gains more control."
Visual: family icon shrinks, chain links appear, government building grows.

SCENE 12 (50 to 55 s)
Voice: "And the loop starts again."
Visual: the full six-step loop animates once around:
1 Rules, 2 Choices, 3 Costs, 4 Less able, 5 Depend, 6 Weaker, then back to 1.

SCENE 13 (55 to 60 s)
Voice: "That is the hypothesis. Now it needs data to test it."
Visual: the loop stays on screen and keeps turning slowly.
Small footer: "Sources: USDA FNS SNAP FY2026 allotments; USDA Cost of Raising a Child;
Roll, Miller and Despard, WashU CSD 2025."
```
