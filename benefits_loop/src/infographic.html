Key numbers

A child costs far more than the benefit adds

$200 a month, at most, in added SNAP food aid
$860 a month to raise a child
SNAP: FY2026, one more person, only at zero income. Child cost: USDA estimate for a lower-income family, 2015 dollars, higher today.

The income limit grows with family size

The SNAP income limit rises about $600 a month per added person, so a bigger family can stay eligible at the same income.

Some workers hold back to keep benefits

About 1 in 5 low-wage workers on benefits say they limit work or savings to stay eligible.
WashU survey, 2025
